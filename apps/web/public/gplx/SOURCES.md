# GPLX illustration sources

## Road signs (`signs/`)

Primary source: **Wikimedia Commons** diagrams of Vietnamese road signs (QCVN 41 / BGTVT), including files tagged **PD-VietnamGov** (official government works as public domain) and community SVG recreations of regulatory sign faces.

Examples:
- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:PD-VietnamGov_(road_signs)
- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Diagrams_of_road_signs_of_Vietnam

Downloaded via Commons `Special:FilePath` for educational use in this GPLX trainer.

Educational recreation (not copied from Commons) when a file was missing:
- `P_124a.svg` — Cấm rẽ phải (QCVN-style prohibitory face)

## Situations (`situations/`)

Original educational SVG diagrams created for this product (not the copyrighted official 600-question exam artwork). Used to illustrate right-of-way, signals, crossings, etc.

## Do not

- Do not scrape or redistribute official Bộ Công an 600-question exam images.
- Prefer Commons PD / QCVN diagrams or original educational art.
